Profile

I am interested in the interplay between human physiology and cellular immunology: how are our immune cells affected by dynamic changes in our physiology? Some of those changes we choose, through diet, exercise, sleep, social interaction and stress. Others are temporal, from rhythmic fluctuations across the day–night cycle to the changes that come with age. My current focus is on biological rhythms and T cell immunity against cancer, and on engineering better adoptive T cell therapies. I am equally committed to meta-science and research rigour, and am exploring company formation as a solo biotech founder.
