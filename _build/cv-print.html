Profile

I'm a T cell biologist interested in where human physiology meets cellular immunology: how do dynamic changes in the body change the way our immune cells work? Some of those changes we choose — diet, exercise, sleep, stress. Others we don't — the daily clock, and the slow arc of ageing. My current focus is biological rhythms and T cell immunity against cancer, and engineering better adoptive T cell therapies out of it.

I care just as much about meta-science and research rigour, which I write about as a 2026 Roots of Progress Fellow, and I'm working towards a company as a solo biotech founder.
